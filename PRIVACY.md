# Widgetly privacy policy

_Last updated: November 4, 2026_

Widgetly makes home screen widgets. It's built to keep your things on your
phone.

## What stays on your phone

- **Photos, GIFs and video clips** you add are copied into Widgetly's private
  storage on your phone (a clip as still frames, without sound). They are
  never uploaded.
- **Designs, profiles and settings** are stored on your phone only.
- **Step counts** are read from your phone's step sensor and stored on your
  phone only.
- **Health data (Pro, only if you connect it):** health widgets read your
  steps, distance, active calories, exercise time, sleep, resting heart rate
  and weight from Health Connect, the place on your phone where Google Health,
  Samsung Health, Oura and other apps keep them. Widgetly only reads
  the kinds you allow, only to show them on your widgets, and keeps the
  latest numbers on your phone. Health data never leaves your phone: it isn't
  sent to us or anyone, isn't in backups, and is never used for ads. You can
  change or remove access any time in Health Connect, or tap Disconnect in
  Widgetly's settings.
- **Calendar events** are read from your phone's calendars to show on widgets
  you add them to. They are never copied off the phone.
- **Music:** music widgets read the song title, artist and cover of what's
  playing, to show them. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
- **Backups and shared designs** are files you choose to create and send. They
  go only where you send them.

## What leaves your phone

- **Google Drive backup (Pro, only if you turn it on):** Widgetly saves a
  backup of your designs, photos and widget settings to your own Google
  Drive, in a hidden folder that only Widgetly can open (Drive's "app data"
  folder). It uses your Drive storage. Widgetly asks Google only for access
  to that folder, never to your other files, and we never see the backups:
  they go straight from your phone to your Drive. Turning backup off stops
  new backups; to delete the old ones, remove Widgetly's data in Google
  Drive's settings (Manage apps).

- **Couple photos and doodles (Pro):** a photo or doodle you send to your
  person is stored, small and in place of the last one you sent, in the
  same database as couple notes, where only the two paired phones can read it.

- **Friend groups:** if you start or join a friend group, your chosen name, an
  anonymous ID, and the latest photo and caption you send are stored in
  Widgetly's Firebase database (Google), in place of the last one you sent,
  so the group's phones can show it. Only people in the group can see them.
  Leaving the group removes them.

- **Pets:** a pet lives on your phone. If you raise it together with a code,
  the pet (its name, kind, needs, hat and scene), who looked after it last,
  and the names and anonymous IDs of the people raising it are stored in
  Widgetly's Firebase database (Google), so everyone's phones can show it.
  Only the people raising it can see it. So that your pets come back after
  you reinstall Widgetly or change phones, a copy of them with their coins,
  streak and items is also stored there under a random save code that only
  your phone knows (Google keeps that code with your phone's own backup);
  it holds no account, email or contact details, and each save replaces the
  last. If you sign in with Google on the pet's Settings tab (optional), the
  same copy is also kept as one small file in your own Google Drive, in the
  hidden folder only Widgetly can open, so signing in on another phone brings
  your pets back. That file is not on our servers; Stop saving stops it and
  Delete my save removes it. Your game scores stay on your phone unless you join the leaderboards: then
  the name you pick and your best score in each game are stored there for
  other players to see, until you leave the leaderboards. If you send your
  pet to visit someone you know, your name and your pet's name and kind are
  stored for them until your next visit replaces it. Pet reminders are made on your phone, not sent from a
  server.

- **Invites:** to give you and your friends a free week of Pro, Widgetly
  stores in its Firebase database a scrambled code made from your phone's
  Android ID (a one-way hash: it can't be turned back into the ID, and it
  says nothing about who you are), your 6-letter invite code, an anonymous
  ID, and when an invite was used. It's used only to count friends who
  joined and to stop the same phone using an invite twice. If you were
  invited, Google Play tells Widgetly which invite link you came from.

- **Usage counts:** Widgetly counts how many times a day features are used
  (for example "a friend photo was sent", "the Pro page was opened" or "the app
  crashed, with this kind of error"),
  added up across everyone, in Widgetly's Firebase database. The counts have
  no ID, name, photo or anything else about you or your phone.

- **Weather:** if you add a weather widget, Widgetly sends the coordinates of
  the city you picked (not your location) to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to
  get the forecast. Searching for a city sends the name you type to
  Open-Meteo's geocoding service. See Open-Meteo's terms for how they handle
  requests.

- **Couple widgets:** if you pair with someone, the hearts and notes you send,
  your chosen name and an anonymous ID are stored in Widgetly's Firebase
  database (Google) so your partner's phone can fetch them. Only the two
  paired phones can read them. Unpairing stops it.

- **Bug reports and ideas:** if you send a report from Help, it includes what
  you typed (name, email and message) and details about the app and your phone
  to help fix problems: app version, phone model, Android version, language,
  screen size, which Widgetly widgets you use, which permissions Widgetly has,
  and, for bug reports, the technical details of the app's last crash. It
  never includes your photos, contacts, calendar events or anything shown on
  your widgets. Reports are delivered to the developer by email through
  Formspree (formspree.io), used only to answer you and fix the app. You can
  send two a day.

- **Theme links:** when you send a theme as a link, the link holds only the
  theme's name, or for a theme made from your photo, its colors, font and
  quote style. The photo itself never leaves your phone. Opening a link
  loads a page on GitHub Pages (github.io), which, like any website, sees
  your IP address.

- **Ads (free version only):** the free version shows ads from Google AdMob:
  a small banner on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad, and videos
  you choose to watch to unlock a theme. AdMob may collect your phone's
  advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address and how you
  interact with ads, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud, as described
  in Google's policy (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). In the
  EU, UK and Switzerland you're asked for consent first, and you can change
  it under Settings, Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your
  advertising ID in your phone's settings. Widgetly Pro has no ads, and
  nothing goes to AdMob.

- **Purchases:** Widgetly Pro is bought through Google Play, which handles
  payment. Widgetly only learns whether you have Pro, never your payment
  details.

That's all. Widgetly has no accounts, and no analytics beyond the anonymous usage counts above.

## Permissions

Each is asked for only when you turn on the feature that needs it:

- **Photos:** through Android's photo picker, which shares only the photos you
  pick. Only if you use an "On this day" widget, Widgetly also asks to read
  your photos, to find ones taken on today's date in earlier years. They're
  shown on the widget and never copied or sent anywhere.
- **Calendar:** to show your events on calendar widgets.
- **Physical activity:** to count steps for step widgets.
- **Internet:** for weather.
- **Notification access:** only if you add a music widget. Android shares
  what's playing only with apps that have this access. Widgetly uses it for
  media controls only and never reads your notifications.
- **Usage access:** only if you add a screen time or data use widget, to
  total today's screen time and data. It never leaves your phone.
- **Contacts:** only if you add a birthdays widget, to read the birthdays
  saved in your contacts. Nothing is copied or sent anywhere.
- **Notifications:** for to-do reminders you set, news about new themes and
  widgets, and a reminder before a free trial ends. Each kind can be turned
  off in Settings.
- **Apps on your phone:** shortcut widgets list your apps so you can pick one
  to open.

## Ratings

Once, after you've set up a few widgets, Widgetly may ask Google Play to show
its rating card. Google Play shows it (or not) and handles your rating;
Widgetly never sees it.

## Deleting your data

Everything on your phone is deleted when you uninstall Widgetly. Leaving a shared pet removes you from it. To have your
couple messages, a shared pet or a report you sent deleted, ask through Help in the app (or
email widgetly.support@gmail.com) and they'll be deleted within
30 days.

## Contact

Questions: use Help in the app, or email widgetly.support@gmail.com.
