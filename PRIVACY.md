# Widgetly privacy policy

_Last updated: October 21, 2026_

Widgetly makes home screen widgets. It's built to keep your things on your
phone.

## What stays on your phone

- **Photos, GIFs and video clips** you add are copied into Widgetly's private
  storage on your phone (a clip as still frames, without sound). They are
  never uploaded.
- **Designs, profiles and settings** are stored on your phone only.
- **Step counts** are read from your phone's step sensor and stored on your
  phone only.
- **Calendar events** are read from your phone's calendars to show on widgets
  you add them to. They are never copied off the phone.
- **Music:** music widgets read the song title, artist and cover of what's
  playing, to show them. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
- **Backups and shared designs** are files you choose to create and send. They
  go only where you send them.

## What leaves your phone

- **Weather:** if you add a weather widget, Widgetly sends the coordinates of
  the city you picked (not your location) to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to
  get the forecast. Searching for a city sends the name you type to
  Open-Meteo's geocoding service. See Open-Meteo's terms for how they handle
  requests.

- **Couple widgets:** if you pair with someone, the hearts and notes you send,
  your chosen name and an anonymous ID are stored in Widgetly's Firebase
  database (Google) so your partner's phone can fetch them. Only the two
  paired phones can read them. Unpairing stops it.

- **Bug reports and ideas:** if you send a report from Help, it includes what
  you typed (name, email and message) and details about the app and your phone
  to help fix problems: app version, phone model, Android version, language,
  screen size, which Widgetly widgets you use, which permissions Widgetly has,
  and, for bug reports, the technical details of the app's last crash. It
  never includes your photos, contacts, calendar events or anything shown on
  your widgets. Reports are delivered to the developer by email through
  Formspree (formspree.io), used only to answer you and fix the app. You can
  send two a day.

That's all. Widgetly has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Permissions

Each is asked for only when you turn on the feature that needs it:

- **Photos:** through Android's photo picker, which shares only the photos you
  pick. Only if you use an "On this day" widget, Widgetly also asks to read
  your photos, to find ones taken on today's date in earlier years. They're
  shown on the widget and never copied or sent anywhere.
- **Calendar:** to show your events on calendar widgets.
- **Physical activity:** to count steps for step widgets.
- **Internet:** for weather.
- **Notification access:** only if you add a music widget. Android shares
  what's playing only with apps that have this access. Widgetly uses it for
  media controls only and never reads your notifications.
- **Usage access:** only if you add a screen time or data use widget, to
  total today's screen time and data. It never leaves your phone.
- **Contacts:** only if you add a birthdays widget, to read the birthdays
  saved in your contacts. Nothing is copied or sent anywhere.
- **Notifications:** only if you set a to-do reminder, to show it.
- **Apps on your phone:** shortcut widgets list your apps so you can pick one
  to open.

## Ratings

Once, after you've set up a few widgets, Widgetly may ask Google Play to show
its rating card. Google Play shows it (or not) and handles your rating;
Widgetly never sees it.

## Deleting your data

Everything on your phone is deleted when you uninstall Widgetly. To have your
couple messages or a report you sent deleted, ask through Help in the app (or
email the address on Widgetly's Google Play page) and they'll be deleted within
30 days.

## Contact

Questions: use Help in the app, or email the developer listed on Google Play.
